The Problem

  • Housing is expensive and isolating for many—especially seniors, students, and single parents
  • Millions of older adults have extra space but feel lonely or financially stretched
  • Younger people struggle to find affordable, safe, and welcoming places to live
  • Current roommate platforms are impersonal and don't address trust or intergenerational needs

The Solution

  • Trusted platform for intergenerational co-living
  • Smart matching, secure messaging, and background checks
  • Focus on compatibility, trust, and community

Market Opportunity

  • 35M+ seniors in the US live alone
  • 20M+ students and young professionals seek affordable housing annually
  • By 2030, 1 in 5 Americans will be 65+
  • Growing demand for affordable, community-based living

How It Works

  • Users create detailed profiles
  • Smart matching algorithm suggests compatible co-living partners
  • Secure messaging, background checks, and video introductions
  • Listings for available rooms/homes
  • Ongoing support and community features

Business Model

  • Freemium: Free to browse, premium features (background checks, priority placement, video chat)
  • Listing fees for homeowners
  • Optional subscription for enhanced matching/support
  • Partnerships with senior centers, universities, and housing organizations
