How PairaLife Works for Homeowners

Earn income from your extra space and enjoy companionship with carefully matched housemates

How PairaLife Helps Homeowners

Our platform simplifies finding compatible housemates for your extra space

Step 1

List Your Home

Create a detailed profile of your home, the space available, and what you're looking for in an ideal housemate.

Our guided listing process helps you showcase your home's best features and communicate your preferences to find the right match.

Step 2

Connect with Potential Housemates

We'll match you with potential housemates who align with your lifestyle, preferences, and house rules.

Our compatibility algorithm considers factors like daily routines, interests, and values to ensure harmonious living arrangements.

Step 3

Interview Safely

Use our secure messaging platform to get to know potential housemates before meeting in person. All users are verified through our multi-step verification process.

We provide guidelines for hosting home visits and questions to ask to ensure you find the right match for your home.

Step 4

Finalize Your Arrangement

Once you've found your match, use our co-living agreement templates to create clear expectations around rent, utilities, common spaces, and house rules.

We provide guidance on fair pricing, security deposits, and how to structure agreements that protect both parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from homeowners about sharing their space

Do I need to modify my home before listing it?

While modifications aren't required, we recommend ensuring the living space offers adequate privacy and safety. Simple changes like adding a lock to the bedroom door or creating a small kitchenette can make your space more appealing to potential housemates.

How does PairaLife ensure my safety?

Safety is our top priority. All users undergo verification checks including identity verification, background checks, and reference reviews. Our messaging system lets you get to know potential matches before meeting. We provide guidance on how to interview housemates and establish clear boundaries.

Will this affect my home insurance or taxes?

We recommend contacting your insurance provider to discuss how home sharing might affect your policy. Some homeowners may need to adjust their coverage. Regarding taxes, rental income is generally taxable, but you may also qualify for certain deductions. We provide resources to help you understand these considerations.

What if the arrangement doesn't work out?

Our co-living agreement templates include clear terms for ending the arrangement, including notice periods and conditions. We also offer mediation services if issues arise. Most arrangements can be adjusted with open communication, which we facilitate through our platform.

How much should I charge for rent?

We provide guidance on setting fair rates based on your location, the space offered, included amenities, and any additional benefits (like meals or transportation). You can also adjust pricing based on any help or assistance the housemate provides as part of the arrangement.
