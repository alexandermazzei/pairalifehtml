How PairaLife Works

Discover our simple process for creating successful intergenerational living arrangements

Our Simple 4-Step Process

PairaLife makes it easy to find your ideal co-living match and create a living arrangement that benefits everyone

Step 1

Create Your Profile

Start by building your detailed profile. Share information about yourself, your lifestyle, your home or housing needs, and what you're looking for in a co-living arrangement.

Our comprehensive profile questions are designed to capture the details that matter in shared living situations, from daily routines to preferences about shared spaces.

Step 2

Get Matched

Our proprietary matching algorithm analyzes numerous compatibility factors to suggest potential matches based on your profile, preferences, and needs.

We consider practical factors like location and timing, but also deeper compatibility factors like lifestyle, values, and expectations for shared living.

Step 3

Connect Safely

When you find a potential match, connect through our secure messaging platform. Get to know each other through text messaging, and eventually video chats if both parties are comfortable.

We provide guidance on what questions to ask and conversation topics to explore to help determine if the match is right for you.

Step 4

Create Your Arrangement

Once you've found your match, we help formalize your co-living arrangement. Use our templates to create clear agreements about house rules, responsibilities, financial arrangements, and more.

We also provide ongoing support with tools for managing shared expenses, scheduling, and resolving any issues that might arise.
