Our Safety Process

Your safety is our top priority at PairaLife

Core Safety Features

How we create a secure environment for everyone in our community

Comprehensive Verification

Every user on PairaLife goes through our multi-step verification process to confirm their identity and background.

We verify government-issued IDs, email addresses, phone numbers, and conduct background screenings to ensure our community consists of trusted individuals.

Secure Communication

Our platform features secure messaging that allows you to get to know potential matches before sharing personal contact information.

All communications are encrypted and monitored for inappropriate content, providing a safe space to establish comfort and compatibility.

Standardized Agreements

Our lawyer-reviewed co-living agreement templates help establish clear expectations and protect both parties legally.

These agreements cover important aspects like rent, utilities, shared spaces, guest policies, notice periods, and conflict resolution procedures.

24/7 Support & Mediation

Our support team is available round-the-clock to assist with any issues that may arise during your co-living experience.

We offer mediation services to help resolve conflicts professionally and maintain harmonious living arrangements for all parties involved.

Our Verification Process

Every PairaLife member completes these steps before joining our community

1
Profile Creation & Email Verification

Users create detailed profiles and verify their email address to ensure active communication channels.

2
Phone Number Verification

We verify phone numbers through SMS confirmation to ensure users can be contacted when needed.

3
ID Verification

Users submit government-issued photo ID which is verified through our secure third-party verification service.

4
Background Screening

We conduct background checks to identify potential issues in criminal history, with transparency about what we check.

5
Reference Checks

For added security, we may request and verify personal or professional references, especially for long-term arrangements.

6
Verification Badge

Once all steps are completed, users receive a "Verified" badge on their profile, indicating they've passed our safety checks.

Safety Tips & Best Practices

Recommendations to ensure a safe and positive co-living experience

Video Chat First

Before meeting in person, have a video call to verify the person matches their profile and establish initial rapport.

Meet in Public Places

For initial meetings, choose public locations like cafes or parks. Inform a friend or family member about your meeting details.

Ask the Right Questions

We provide suggested question lists to help you learn important information about potential housemates or homeowners.

Home Visit Guidelines

For homeowners showing spaces, invite a friend or family member to be present during tours with new potential housemates.

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong or uncomfortable, it's okay to step back. Your safety and comfort are the highest priority.

Protect Personal Information

Only share sensitive personal or financial information after you've established trust and are ready to finalize arrangements.

Frequently Asked Safety Questions

Common questions about safety on PairaLife

How extensive are your background checks?

Our background checks include identity verification, address history, criminal records at national and county levels, sex offender registry screening, and global watchlist searches. Users are informed about the checks and must provide consent.

What happens if I have a safety concern about a user?

We take all safety concerns seriously. You can report any suspicious activity or safety concerns through our platform, and our safety team will investigate promptly. In emergency situations, always contact local authorities first.

Are my personal details and verification documents secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data. Verification documents are only accessed by our verification team and our trusted third-party verification service. We never share this information with other users.

What safety features are in place for financial transactions?

For initial payments and deposits, we offer a secure payment platform that protects both parties. We also provide guidance on safe payment methods for ongoing rent payments. Never send money to someone you haven't met in person or verified through our platform.

How do you handle disputes between housemates?

We offer a mediation service to help resolve conflicts before they escalate. Our team can facilitate discussions between parties and provide guidance based on the agreements established. We also have resources available for conflict resolution strategies.
