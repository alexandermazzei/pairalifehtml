Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I create an account?

To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner of the homepage. You'll need to provide your email address, create a password, and verify your email. After that, you'll be guided through the profile creation process.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes, we take data privacy very seriously. Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We only share your information with other users according to your privacy settings, and we never sell your data to third parties. You can learn more in our Privacy Policy.

How does the verification process work?

Our verification process includes email verification, phone number verification, and ID verification. For ID verification, you'll need to upload a government-issued ID, which will be checked by our secure verification partner. This helps create a trusted community where everyone is who they say they are.

What are the subscription options?

We offer three subscription tiers: Basic, Premium, and Pro. Each tier offers different features and benefits, such as advanced matching, unlimited messaging, and priority support. You can view the details and pricing of each subscription on our Pricing page or in your Account Settings.
