About Me

Hi there! I'm a 35-year-old software engineer currently working remotely for a tech company. I've been living in San Francisco for over 10 years and absolutely love this city. I've had wonderful experiences sharing my home with roommates over the years and believe that co-living can be a fantastic way to not only share costs but also build meaningful connections.

I'm a clean, organized person who respects shared spaces and privacy. When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new restaurants, hiking in nearby parks, or curled up with a good book.

I enjoy hosting people who are respectful, reasonably tidy, and open to occasional shared meals or conversations, though I completely understand the need for personal space and alone time. Looking forward to connecting with potential housemates!
