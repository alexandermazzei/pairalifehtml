Meet Our Team

The passionate people behind PairaLife's mission to transform shared living

Who We Are

At PairaLife, our team brings together diverse expertise across technology, real estate, social services, and community building. We're united by a commitment to create innovative housing solutions that foster connection across generations.

Each team member contributes unique perspectives and skills, but we all share a passion for transforming how people think about shared living arrangements and intergenerational connections.
